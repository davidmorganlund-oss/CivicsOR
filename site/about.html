About CivicsOR

One place to see what Oregon's public bodies are deciding, and how to take part.

Who runs it

CivicsOR is a service of BarNone, Inc., an independent nonprofit. It is not a government website. It is not affiliated with any government agency, and it does not speak for any public office. It doesn't take sides on candidates, parties, bills or ballot measures.

Where the meetings come from

Every listing comes from a public body's own published calendar: city and county meeting portals, school district calendars, library schedules, regional governments like Metro, and the Oregon Legislature's committee schedule. Each night, CivicsOR reads those calendars and updates the site. Every meeting links back to the host's own page.

A few public bodies publish a standing schedule ("the second and fourth Tuesday at 10 a.m.") instead of a calendar. Those meetings are marked Regular schedule: confirm first, because they can be cancelled or moved without the change reaching us. When a standing schedule lands on a state holiday, we leave that date off.

How current it is

Listings refresh every night. If a calendar can't be reached, we keep showing what it listed last time and keep trying. The coverage page shows every calendar we read and when each was last read successfully.

Always confirm with the host before you go. Agendas, times and rooms change, sometimes the same day.

What's not here yet

Some public bodies only post PDFs, or don't post a calendar online at all. CivicsOR reads public meeting notices once a night with a reader that identifies itself, and keeps its requests light. Where a calendar can't be read, the site links to the body's own meeting page instead. The coverage page lists what's included county by county. Spanish pages are planned.

Found a mistake or a missing meeting?

Report a correction

Email admin@barnoneinc.org. Tell us what's wrong and where, and include a link to the right information if you have one.

Privacy

CivicsOR has no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and it doesn't sell or share information about visitors. The county you pick is saved only in your own browser. Read the privacy page.

Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Pages work with a keyboard and screen readers, text meets contrast standards, and the site works on phones. If something gets in your way, please tell us and we'll fix it.
