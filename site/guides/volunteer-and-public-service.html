Volunteering and public service jobs

Some of the most direct ways to take part don't involve a vote at all. Here is how to find volunteer roles and public jobs in Oregon.

Volunteer

  • OregonServes, the state's service commission, supports AmeriCorps programs across Oregon and promotes volunteering and civic engagement.
  • Local governments use volunteers in parks, libraries, senior centers and emergency preparedness programs, including Community Emergency Response Teams. Check your city or county website.
  • Elections. County elections offices hire temporary workers and welcome observers around each election.
  • Boards and committees are volunteer service too. See serve on a board or commission.

Give wisely

  • Check a charity before you give. The Oregon Department of Justice's Charitable Activities Section keeps a searchable registry of charities with their financial reports.
  • Small community groups often do the most with the least.

Public service jobs

  • State jobs are posted on the State of Oregon's jobs site.
  • Federal jobs are posted on USAJOBS.
  • City, county, school district and special district jobs are posted on each government's own website.
  • Internships are offered by the Legislature, state agencies and many local governments.

Public jobs range from engineers and nurses to planners, mechanics, analysts and clerks, and many qualify for the federal Public Service Loan Forgiveness program.
