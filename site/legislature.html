What the agenda words mean

Public hearing. The committee takes public testimony on the bill. This is your chance to speak, in person or remotely, or to send written testimony.

Work session. The committee discusses the bill and may vote on amendments and on sending it to the floor. There's usually no public testimony, but you can watch.

Informational meeting. A briefing for the committee from agencies, experts or invited guests. No votes.

How to testify

  1. Find the bill on a committee's agenda. Registration opens once a public hearing is scheduled.
  2. On the bill's OLIS page, choose Register to Testify, in person or remotely by video or phone. Registration closes 30 minutes before the meeting starts.
  3. Or choose Submit Testimony to send it in writing, up to 48 hours after the meeting's scheduled start.
  4. Keep it short: who you are, where you live, and how the bill would affect you.
