How to testify

  1. Find the bill on a committee's agenda below. Agendas usually post a few days ahead.
  2. Open the bill or committee on OLIS and choose Register to Testify, in person or remotely. Registration closes shortly before the meeting.
  3. Or choose Submit Testimony to send it in writing, up to 48 hours after the hearing's scheduled start.
  4. Keep it short: who you are, where you live, and how the bill would affect you.
